## Interpretation and identity rules

- Count values are taken from the product name or its explicit count-labelled category. Aida/canvas counts are stitches per inch; linen/evenweave counts are fabric threads per inch. Default cross-stitch spacing is over one for Aida/canvas and over two for linen/evenweave, and remains editable. This default is a stitching assumption, not a physical property or a guarantee that every stitch technique uses it.
- Physical dimensions are never inferred from a colour name, photo or marketing description. Fabrics/supports without a declared count require the user to supply it before dimension calculations.
- Codes remain strings. Explicit DMC Mouliné aliases bridge the bundled MakeBead palette’s `01`–`35` labels to the retailer’s `001`–`035` labels; `BLANC`/`Blanc`/`White` map to the Italian `Bianco` product. This mapping is limited to the DMC Mouliné range, never a generic removal of leading zeroes.
- An empty DMC range matches only when the explicit bundled provenance is `catalogueRef.name === "DMC · MakeBead"`. Other materials without a range stay unresolved. Caron spelling/brand and Kreinik #4 spelling aliases are bounded explicit aliases.
- The bundled DMC palette has one unmatched code, `776`. The retailer lists `3326` as a replacement, which is retained as a retailer note, never silently treated as the same material or stock.
- Search results and matching return recorded product objects. Unknown, incompatible or ambiguous identities return no purchase link. Product records are frozen; selection does not mutate the snapshot.

The preset contains reference metadata and 35 material requirements: 26 DMC colours, Caron Waterlilies 253 Ink, Kreinik #4 018HL and 052F, and six Mill Hill bead references. Thread/skein/spool quantities are unknown because the retrieved product list does not expose verified quantities. Bead pack requirements are 00020 ×1, 00479 ×2, 02026 ×1, 03041 ×1, **10101 ×1**, and 80479 ×2. The publisher explicitly corrects 10101 from two packs to one. No approximate bead count per pack is converted into an exact inventory quantity.

The publisher gives 230 ×190 stitches on 32-count Laurel linen, over two fabric threads, which calculates to 36.5125 ×30.1625 cm before margins. The stated alternative is 16-count Sage/Summer Khaki Aida. The exact Casa Cenina Laurel Vintage page reports unavailable/cancelled in its retrieved snapshot; its link is preserved as a reference, without promising an available purchase.

The actual chart is not present on these product pages. The preset contains no chart cells, PDF or copied artwork. Users import their own chart file.
